Cream City Data is a two-founder company built around a simple idea: data and AI should help people do meaningful work.

We care about the entire journey, from reliable data foundations to clear analysis and thoughtfully evaluated AI. Our approach is to start with a real question, build understanding, and choose tools that fit the problem.

## What matters to us

- **Useful over flashy.** Start with the decision or workflow that needs to improve.
- **Trust from the ground up.** Data quality, clear definitions, and maintainable systems matter.
- **Curiosity with rigor.** Experiment openly, measure outcomes, and learn from what does not work.
- **Responsible AI.** Consider privacy, limitations, and human oversight from the beginning.
